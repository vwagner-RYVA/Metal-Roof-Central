  • Fasten any loose roofing material.
  • Seal all penetrations.
  • Seal chimneys.
  • Seal skylights.
  • Check valleys and seal where necessary.
  • Check all flashings and seal where necessary.
  • Inspect ventilation. Tighten and seal where necessary.

About our roofing, our process, and our training program.

Stone-coated metal roofing uses steel panels coated with natural stone chips, giving the look of tile or shake with the strength and lifespan of steel. Modular metal roofing uses precision-engineered interlocking panels for a faster, more consistent installation. Our collection is modular metal roofing in metal shingle, shake, and tile profiles.

A properly installed metal roof typically lasts 40 to 70 years or more, compared with roughly 15 to 30 years for asphalt shingles.

Our roof systems carry a 50-year warranty. The warranty is transferable to the next owner of the property, which makes a metal roof a sound long-term investment if you plan to sell. Terms and conditions apply.

The upfront cost is typically higher than asphalt shingles, but a metal roof's longevity, energy savings, and low maintenance can make it the more economical choice over the life of the home.
