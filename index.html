Frequently Asked Questions

Questions? We've got answers

About our roofing, our process, and our training program.

Stone-coated metal roofing uses steel panels coated with natural stone chips, giving the look of tile or shake shingles with the strength and lifespan of steel. Modular metal roofing uses precision-engineered interlocking panels for a faster, more consistent installation. We install both, along with metal shingles and tiles.

A properly installed metal roof can last 40-70+ years — significantly longer than traditional asphalt shingles, which typically need replacement every 15-20 years.

The upfront cost is typically higher than asphalt shingles, but metal roofing's lifespan, energy savings, and lower maintenance needs make it more cost-effective over time.

Yes. Metal roofing systems are built and rated for high-wind and hurricane-prone regions, offering far greater wind and impact resistance than standard shingles.

Metal roofing reflects solar heat rather than absorbing it, which can meaningfully reduce cooling costs compared to traditional roofing materials.
